Selection rule

A change is kept only if it beats the current best on every noisy set (synthetic, real noise, silver), including the private-like re-weighting of impostor pairs. Scoring fixes apply only inside the segment where errors concentrate.

Why not EER 5

Of our errors on real noise: 23% mislabelled trials, 39% also missed by the 12.5M teacher, 38% capacity (fixing all = teacher level 5.25). Only transductive scoring has moved EER that far: a rules question for the organizers.
